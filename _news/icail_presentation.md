It was my second time attending ICAIL and first time presenting there! There were also some fantastic workshops I attended such as [ASAIL 2025](https://sites.google.com/view/asail/asail-home?authuser=0).

My work "Curb Your Enthusiasm: Towards a RAG Framework for Forecasting Case Importance in the ECtHR" proposes an initial RAG framework for examining the task of predicting how legally significant a case that has been submitted to the ECtHR may be, using communicated cases (data available before any proceedings or decisions) and prior case law. The full paper will be available shortly in the [conference proceedings](https://dl.acm.org/conference/icail/proceedings).
